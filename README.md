      <p>
        Shopee is one of the leading e-commerce companies, currently running in 10 major markets across Southeast Asia, East Asia (including Taiwan), and Latin America. With that kind of scale, competition on the platform gets brutal. From a buyer's perspective, having so many product choices often just makes decision-making harder. So people end up leaning heavily on psychological triggers like discount size, free shipping promos, and store reputation before they actually hit "buy". That means sellers can't just list products and hope for the best anymore. They need a real strategy to stand out in a sea of competitors.
      </p>

        To address these challenges, I used 19 days of market data from Shopee Taiwan, covering April to May 2026. The dataset is huge, with over 19,000 unique products from roughly 13,000 different stores. It includes key commercial metrics like price range, discount levels, seller tier, and customer interest signals such as like counts.
      </p>
